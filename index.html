How a project runs

Five steps, usually inside a week.

  1. BriefProduct, audience, mood and where the image will run.
  2. PromptScene, lighting, lens and colors written as a clear spec.
  3. GenerateBatches reviewed for label accuracy and realism.
  4. RefineRetouch, fix typography, match the packaging.
  5. DeliverFinal files sized for each channel.

A prompt, for example

Amber pump bottle with coral label on a wet shower ledge, soft window light from the left, water droplets on glass, shallow depth of field, solid olive green background (#4d523c), 85mm look, 9:16, label text sharp and unchanged.
